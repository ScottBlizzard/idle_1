## 3. Certificate theorem audit: what can and cannot be rescued

### 3.1 Frozen compositional estimand

For record \(r\), system \(s\in\{\mathrm{PAT},\mathrm{TAR}\}\), and selected gate \(k\in\{1,\ldots,10\}\), let

\[
P_{rsk}=G_{rsk}g_{rsk}^{\top}
\]

be the matched-bypass local path operator, with response-only estimate

\[
\widehat P_{rsk}=\widehat G_{rsk}\widehat g_{rsk}^{\top}.
\]

For the frozen output contrast \(c_r\) and physical direction \(v_r\), define the scalar contribution

\[
z_{rsk}=c_r^{\top}P_{rsk}v_r,
\qquad
\widehat z_{rsk}=c_r^{\top}\widehat P_{rsk}v_r.
\]

With \(\sigma_{\mathrm{PAT}}=+1\) and \(\sigma_{\mathrm{TAR}}=-1\), the joint target and response-only compositional center are

\[
\theta_r=\sum_{s,k}\sigma_s z_{rsk},
\qquad
\widehat\theta_r=\sum_{s,k}\sigma_s\widehat z_{rsk}.
\]

### 3.2 The current box certificate is minimax sharp

Suppose the only certified primitive information is

\[
|\widehat z_i-z_i|\le b_i,\qquad i=1,\ldots,m,
\]

where \(m=20\) for ten PAT and ten TAR contributions. Let \(e_i=\widehat z_i-z_i\) and let \(a_i\in\{+1,-1\}\) encode the PAT/TAR signs. The uncertainty set is the box

\[
K_{\Box}=\prod_{i=1}^{m}[-b_i,b_i].
\]

The worst-case joint error is the support function of that box:

\[
\sup_{e\in K_{\Box}}|a^{\top}e|
=
\sum_{i=1}^{m}|a_i|b_i
=
\sum_{i=1}^{m}b_i.
\]

**Proof.** The upper bound is the triangle inequality. Equality is attained by choosing \(e_i=b_i\operatorname{sign}(a_i)\), and the negative equality is attained by the opposite choice. \(\square\)

This matters: the v3 triangle sum is not merely an inelegant implementation. It is the smallest uniformly valid deterministic radius implied by the v3 product-box assumptions. No algebraic rearrangement of the saved per-gate radii can make it smaller while preserving the same guarantee.

### 3.3 Why observed error correlations do not produce a proof-valid replacement

The v3 centers show substantial cancellation in their realized errors. That does not imply that future errors, or the unknown exact error vector for a given record, belong to a narrower deterministic set. In particular:

- Pearson or Spearman correlation estimates describe sampled centers, not the support of the unknown error vector.
- Estimating covariance from the same 80 records and then using it to shrink those same records is circular.
- A covariance ellipsoid yields a probabilistic statement only after assumptions about exchangeability, centering, tails, clustering, and independent calibration are declared and validated.
- With 20 signed contributions, five development groups, shared model computations, shared directions, and repeated gate/system measurements, a naïve sample covariance is badly underidentified as a simultaneous per-record certificate.

A legitimate ellipsoidal route would require a fresh calibration split and a theorem of the form

\[
\Pr\!\left((e-\mu)^{\top}\Sigma^{-1}(e-\mu)\le q_{1-\alpha}\right)\ge 1-\alpha,
\]

which would imply

\[
|a^{\top}(e-\mu)|\le \sqrt{q_{1-\alpha}\,a^{\top}\Sigma a}.
\]

That would be a high-probability population statement, not the deterministic set certificate frozen in v3. It is scientifically possible as a later secondary analysis, but it is not an admissible rescue of the present verdict and it is not the main successor route.

### 3.4 What the saved artifacts are sufficient to establish

The saved artifacts are sufficient to establish all of the following:

1. the centers are accurate;
2. the box certificate is the bottleneck;
3. the missing projection term is negligible at the observed scale;
4. realized component errors often cancel;
5. the v3 panel is saturated at high identifiability;
6. the point-estimator and AD-target firewalls held.

They are **not** sufficient to establish a smaller deterministic joint uncertainty set. A proof-valid contraction requires a new joint measurement or a new primitive theorem.

### 3.5 Terminal answer to the certificate question

A proof-valid correlation-aware certificate is possible, but only in this precise sense:

> Replace the product of independently certified component intervals by a directly certified interval for the single signed all-gate functional. Treat that interval as a joint slab constraint, and use it to certify the separately computed response-only compositional center.

It is not possible by post hoc covariance shrinkage of the v3 box.

\frac{\Psi(h)-\Psi(-h)}{2h}

## 5. Detectability theory: two regimes, not one global rank law

### 5.1 What the v3 result implies

The v3 panel sampled almost exclusively the regime

\[
\kappa\gg 1,
\]

where \(\kappa\) denotes an effective identification signal-to-uncertainty ratio. In such a regime, estimator error is governed by a residual floor—floating evaluation, finite-radius remainder, frame leakage, or implementation tolerances—rather than by the leading \(1/\kappa\) term. Consequently, increasing \(\kappa\) from 140 to 3,600 need not produce a detectable rank ordering among errors already around \(10^{-6}\).

The failed global Spearman test is binding for v3, but the scientifically correct successor hypothesis is piecewise:

1. **Transition regime:** recovery quality improves as effective SNR crosses the identification boundary.
2. **Plateau regime:** once the leading uncertainty is below the residual floor, a strong global monotone correlation is neither predicted nor required.

### 5.2 Minimal deterministic model

Let an operator estimate under a controlled measurement channel satisfy

\[
\|\widehat P_{\alpha}-P\|
\le
b_0+\frac{b_1}{\alpha},
\qquad 0<\alpha\le1,
\]

where \(b_0\) is the natural approximation floor and \(b_1/\alpha\) is a controlled observation-error term. Define

\[
\kappa_{\alpha}
=
\frac{\|P\|}{b_0+b_1/\alpha}.
\]

Then the relative error obeys

\[
\frac{\|\widehat P_{\alpha}-P\|}{\|P\|}
\le
\kappa_{\alpha}^{-1}.
\]

As \(\alpha\) increases from a small value, \(\kappa_\alpha\) crosses order one and recovery becomes possible. Once \(b_1/\alpha\ll b_0\), both the bound and observed error saturate near \(b_0\). A single Spearman coefficient over only that saturated region is not a test of the transition theorem.

This model motivates the experiment below. It does not excuse the v3 failure and it is not fitted to v3 outcomes.

## 6. Outcome-blind boundary-spanning detectability experiment

### 6.1 Controlled variable

The experiment manipulates only the precision of the saved **downstream finite-response measurement channel**. It does not change:

- the model weights;
- prompts or suffixes;
- selected gates;
- PAT/TAR systems;
- the local path operator \(P\);
- the AD target;
- the physical direction;
- the component estimator formula after the known attenuation is inverted.

For each scalar endpoint difference \(\Delta y=y(t)-y(0)\), define the subtractively dithered quantizer

\[
Q_q^{\xi}(x)
=
q\,\operatorname{round}\!\left(\frac{x+\xi}{q}\right)-\xi,
\qquad
\xi\in[-q/2,q/2).
\]

It obeys the deterministic bound

\[
|Q_q^{\xi}(x)-x|\le q/2.
\]

At attenuation \(\alpha\), the observed endpoint difference is

\[
\widetilde{\Delta y}_{\alpha,q}
=
Q_q^{\xi}(\alpha\Delta y),
\]

and the estimator divides by the known \(\alpha\). With zero quantization error, the derivative estimand is exactly unchanged. With quantization, the added error is known and increases as \(1/\alpha\).

### 6.2 Frozen attenuation grid

The attenuation grid is

\[
\mathcal A=\{2^{-j}:j=0,1,\ldots,16\}.
\]

It is frozen before development. No level may be deleted because its result looks inconvenient, and no new level may be added after development begins.

### 6.3 Outcome-blind quantization-scale selection

Formal prepare uses a completely separate donor panel and the dyadic candidate set

\[
\mathcal Q=\{2^{-52},2^{-50},\ldots,2^{-12}\}
\]

in scalar logit units. For each candidate \(q\), prepare computes the **predicted** effective SNR from certified response magnitudes and the analytic quantization bound; it does not compute or inspect recovery error. Candidates are evaluated in ascending \(q\), and the first candidate satisfying all four conditions is selected:

1. donor median \(\kappa_{\alpha=1}\ge16\);
2. donor median \(\kappa_{\alpha=2^{-8}}\in[2,8]\);
3. donor median \(\kappa_{\alpha=2^{-16}}\le1\);
4. at least 80% of donor groups contain units in the low, transition, and high regimes.

If no candidate satisfies the rule, prepare returns `PREPARE_STOP_BOUNDARY_NOT_SPANNED`. There is no second attempt and no manual q adjustment.

### 6.4 Exact quantization-error propagation

Let a finite-response coordinate use stencil weights \(w_1,\ldots,w_J\). After attenuation inversion, its added scalar error is bounded by

\[
\epsilon_q(\alpha)
\le
\frac{q}{2\alpha}\sum_{j=1}^{J}|w_j|.
\]

For \(d\) independently quantized coordinates collected in a Frobenius norm,

\[
\epsilon_{q,F}(\alpha)
\le
\frac{q\sqrt d}{2\alpha}\sum_{j=1}^{J}|w_j|.
\]

The effective response SNR is recomputed as

\[
\kappa^R_{u,\alpha}
=
\frac{\|G_u\|_F}
{\epsilon^R_{u,0}+\epsilon_{q,F,u}(\alpha)}.
\]

The overall predictor is

\[
\kappa_{u,\alpha}
=
\min\bigl(
\kappa^C_u,
\kappa^P_u,
\kappa^R_{u,\alpha}
\bigr),
\]

where \(\kappa^C\) and \(\kappa^P\) retain the frozen curvature and operator identifiability definitions. Only the response channel is manipulated.

### 6.5 Deterministic dither seeds

Four dither replicates are used per unit and attenuation level. Dither is generated from

```text
SHA256(
  protocol_id | split_sha256 | record_id | system | gate_slot |
  direction_id | endpoint_id | alpha_index | replicate_index
)
```

mapped deterministically to \([-q/2,q/2)\). The seeds are therefore fixed before any model output exists. Replicates are nested observations, never treated as independent groups.

### 6.6 Required endpoint artifact

The GPU worker saves the float64 baseline-subtracted endpoint packet needed to replay every \(\alpha\) and dither replicate offline. The packet must include:

- record, group, cell, system, gate slot, and direction identifiers;
- exact stencil node and radius;
- raw scalar or projected-vector endpoint difference;
- response-envelope terms;
- active-model fingerprint;
- point-estimator-route hash;
- no AD target or witness values.

The boundary sweep must not require new model calls after the natural endpoint packet is sealed.

### 6.7 Regime definitions

For preregistered analysis:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{low SNR} &: \kappa\le1,\\
\text{transition} &: 1<\kappa<16,\\
\text{high-SNR plateau} &: \kappa\ge16.
\end{aligned}
\]

The recovery outcome is the existing direct operator error, with the v3 denominator and floor copied byte-for-byte. There is no replacement metric selected after seeing v4.

### 6.8 Group-balanced primary statistics

For each noun-century group \(g\), compute within the transition regime:

\[
\rho_g
=
\operatorname{Spearman}
\left(
\log_{10}\kappa,
-\log_{10}E
\right),
\]

and a Theil-Sen slope \(\beta_g\) for the same axes. Define group-balanced summaries

\[
\rho_{GB}=\operatorname{median}_g\rho_g,
\qquad
\beta_{GB}=\operatorname{median}_g\beta_g.
\]

For regime separation, define within each group

\[
R_g
=
\frac{\operatorname{median}(E\mid \kappa\le1)}
     {\operatorname{median}(E\mid \kappa\ge16)},
\qquad
R_{GB}=\operatorname{median}_gR_g.
\]

For plateau behavior, estimate \(\beta^{hi}_g\) only on \(\kappa\ge16\), then use the group median.

All confidence intervals resample noun-century groups as the top-level cluster. Records, systems, gates, directions, attenuation levels, and dither replicates remain nested inside their original group.

### 6.9 Scientific interpretation firewall

A successful boundary experiment supports the statement:

> Under a preregistered measurement-channel intervention that preserves the local path-operator estimand, recovery changes sharply as the certified effective SNR crosses the predicted identification boundary, while natural v3-like observations lie on a high-SNR plateau.

It does **not** support the stronger statement that naturally occurring GPT-2 prompts span the same transition, nor that all mechanistic-interpretability estimators obey a universal phase transition.

### 7.5 Three physically separate routes

Every record is evaluated through three process-level roles:

1. **Component route C:** response-only \(\widehat G\), \(\widehat g\), \(\widehat P\), and \(\widehat\theta\).

A worker process may execute exactly one role. No process may retain a model instance and switch roles.

```text
Stage M: CPU-only immutable merge and audit
Stage B: CPU-only boundary-channel replay from sealed endpoint packets
```

The projected operation count must remain below 24 wall-clock hours on 8×RTX 4090, peak allocation below 20 GiB per GPU, and no worker may use TF32. If formal prepare cannot certify the budget from measured donor throughput with a 2× safety factor, it stops.

## 9. Firewalls and anti-overfitting rules

The following rules are binding.

1. **V3 confirmation remains sealed.** It may not become a donor, calibration, development, or confirmation set for v4.
2. **Fresh v4 confirmation remains sealed** until a separate authority explicitly authorizes its opening after a clean development pass.
3. **No threshold changes** after formal prepare starts.
4. **No candidate-list changes** after its SHA-256 is written.
5. **No q or alpha changes** after prepare; no attenuation level may be dropped.
6. **No route leakage.** Component code cannot import witness or AD modules; witness code cannot import AD modules.
7. **No center calibration.** The witness interval cannot shift, regress, or recalibrate the response-only center.
8. **No empirical covariance shrinkage** in the deterministic certificate.
9. **No clipping or trimming** of invalid or contradictory records.
10. **No retry/resume.** A crash after any result is written makes the attempt invalid unless the exact existing protocol already proves byte-identical transactional recovery; v4 does not authorize such recovery.
11. **No model mutation.** Weights, buffers, hook order, tokenizer, and source hashes must match before and after every worker.
12. **No adaptive query allocation** based on interim error, witness width, or AD target.
13. **No confirmation code path** in the currently authorized execution command.
14. **No silent corrigendum.** Any defect produces an incident report and a new scientific decision before rerun.
15. **No narrative rescue.** A failed hard gate cannot be reframed as a pass in the paper.
