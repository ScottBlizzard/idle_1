## 1. Correct scientific frame

The project was deliberately designed as a scientific advance beyond Grant et al., *Addressing Divergent Representations from Causal Interventions on Neural Networks* (ICLR 2026 Oral). Grant et al. is therefore a foundation and adversarial reference, not evidence that GREEN is redundant.

The inherited premise is:

> Causal interventions can create divergent internal representations.

GREEN's target is the harder unresolved regime:

> An intervention can remain behaviorally convincing while silently supporting the wrong, bypassed, or non-identifying mechanism. A useful method must identify that risk prospectively and be validated by a consequence that was not used to construct the warning.

The paper must not claim novelty for the inherited premise. It must claim and test the advance beyond it.

## 3. Correct hierarchy of claims

The scientific hierarchy is now frozen as follows.

1. **Primary scientific question:** can GREEN prospectively identify a silent mechanistic failure that ordinary restoration, patching, attribution, or first/second-order baselines would accept?
2. **Primary empirical endpoint:** an independent held-out causal/mechanistic consequence not consumed by GREEN, P13, the certificate, amplitude construction, or baseline selection.
3. **Secondary diagnostic:** whether the GREEN certificate resolves, remains unresolved, or is invalid.
4. **Technical validation:** whether resolution changes with precision, budget, or a synthetic measurement-channel amplitude.

A transition in certificate resolution alone is not a mechanism discovery and cannot support the Oral claim. It remains useful as a calibrated measurement/power result.

## 4. Statistical correction to inherited section 13.5

The inherited low-amplitude clause used the wrong one-sided confidence bound. To establish that success probability is at most 0.20, the simultaneous 95% **upper** confidence bound must be at most 0.20. A lower confidence bound at most 0.20 does not establish low probability.

The corrected directional gates are:

- low regime: at least one of the two lowest complete bins has simultaneous 95% **UCB <= 0.20**;
- high regime: at least one of the two highest complete bins has simultaneous 95% **LCB >= 0.80**.

This is a mathematical error correction made before real v4 outcomes. It cannot be reverted after outcome access.

## 5. Independence contract for the primary endpoint

Before any real scientific outcome is opened, the primary endpoint must be serialized and hashed. It must satisfy all of the following:

1. Its labels or values are generated by a physically and logically separate causal intervention or transport measurement.
2. It does not use the Joint Witness interval, P13 status, certificate width, adaptive precision/budget history, or the attenuation/dither amplitude in its definition.
3. Its worker does not import the certificate evaluator or baseline-selection code.
4. Candidate selection, prediction, and endpoint adjudication use separate process roles and immutable record identifiers.
5. GREEN predictions and all baseline predictions are committed before endpoint adjudication.
6. The endpoint measures a mechanistic or causal consequence, not agreement with GREEN's own functional.

The existing independent AD route is a numerical adjudicator of the same estimand. It is valuable for implementation validation but does not satisfy this scientific-independence contract by itself.

## 7. Oral-level success and failure boundaries

The Oral-level result requires the following prospective sequence on preregistered untouched records:

1. a standard method makes a confident mechanistic call;
2. GREEN prospectively rejects, leaves unresolved, or predicts a different consequence;
3. the independent held-out endpoint supports GREEN rather than the standard call;
4. the result survives simple power, amplitude, curvature, depth, and compute controls;
5. the result transports to a second task, model, or mechanism family under an unchanged decision rule.

If only certificate resolution improves with amplitude or budget, the result is a rigorous technical validation, not the paper's central mechanistic claim. If GREEN does not improve prediction of the independent endpoint beyond strong baselines, the Oral claim fails without invalidating the theorem, software, or earlier diagnostic evidence.
