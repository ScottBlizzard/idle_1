## Method design risks

1. **Semantic scope:** the theorem certifies a declared path functional, not a unique true mechanism. The draft states this consistently.
2. **Panel scope:** eight directions can miss discrepancies outside their span. The draft does not claim full-space equivalence.
3. **Runtime scope:** exact-real graph certification is not automatically a bit-level proof of arbitrary PyTorch execution. Trace and replay audits remain separate.
4. **Power confounding:** certificate status may track signal or compute difficulty. Frozen matched controls must decide this.
5. **Net value:** deterministic certification is expensive. Its scientific value depends on outperforming cheaper baselines at useful coverage.
