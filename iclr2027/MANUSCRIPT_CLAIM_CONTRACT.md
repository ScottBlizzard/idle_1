# Manuscript claim contract

## One-sentence contribution

We convert a precisely declared matched-bypass transport relation for activation interventions into a deterministic, budget-monotone local certificate on an unmodified Transformer tail, and design a prospective two-task test of whether the certificate predicts held-out response transport beyond restoration and empirical baselines.

## Three contribution levels

1. **Established:** restoration, activation support, local functional agreement, and selected structural-path agreement are distinct evidence objects; earlier attempts to collapse them produced falsifiable failures.
2. **Established method:** a shared relational interval-jet program can soundly enclose the four-branch matched-bypass functional, its derivative, and curvature while preserving exact cancellation; independent higher-precision replay audits rather than constructs the official certificate.
3. **Pending empirical claim:** on untouched IOI and Greater-Than instances, the certified status predicts held-out full-response transport error at useful coverage and beats frozen baselines.
