# ICLR 2027 Narrative Upgrade — 2026-09-02

## One-sentence argument

Behavioral restoration can lie about mechanism: after three natural diagnostic shortcuts fail under targeted falsification, GREEN makes one deliberately narrow local-transport claim, certifies it with a shared deterministic program, commits its status, and only then opens an independent endpoint that can prove whether the certificate is scientifically useful.

## Reviewer-facing story

1. **Phenomenon:** a patch can restore the correct answer without establishing the intended local computation.
2. **Why existing evidence is insufficient:** reference-relative support, response agreement, and a highly accurate center estimate can each fail to certify path-specific transport.
3. **Scientific object:** local matched-bypass transport equivalence for a declared site, direction panel, task contrast, gate set, and tolerance.
4. **Technical idea:** compile the four relational branches into one shared downstream causal-cone program so exact cancellation survives interval propagation; use signed secants for finite-radius derivative bounds and independent high-precision replay for audit.
5. **Prospective test:** commit a five-way GREEN status before opening a sealed full-response endpoint.
6. **Oral-level decision:** GREEN must improve accepted-set risk at matched coverage over already-strong public-panel baselines on the untouched successor. A sound certificate without predictive value is not presented as a scientific win.

## Section spine

- **Introduction:** name the failure mode (“restoration lie”), establish the predictive gap, and state the bounded question.
- **Claims and non-claims:** separate the proved object from mechanism uniqueness and global faithfulness.
- **Method:** define the matched-bypass target, shared cone, interval jets, signed secants, statuses, and audit split.
- **Prospective protocol:** explain public construction versus sealed endpoint and matched-coverage evaluation.
- **Completed evidence:** report the development-only silent-failure diagnostic and the three falsifiers as the derivation of the claim boundary.
- **Confirmation:** reserve one consolidated result figure for status distribution, coverage-risk curves, falsifiers, and the precommitted decision.
- **Discussion:** distinguish intervention causality, relation-level certification, proof sharpness, and external usefulness.

## Terminology ledger

- **Restoration lie:** correct restored behavior without establishment of the intended local mechanistic conclusion.
- **Local matched-bypass transport equivalence:** the exact bounded relational object certified by GREEN.
- **Certificate:** a deterministic statement about that declared object, not a claim of unique mechanism discovery.
- **Status:** one of certified agreement, certified disagreement, unresolved proof, invalid execution, or exhausted compute.
- **Sealed endpoint:** an independently routed full-response consequence hidden until status commitment.
- **Scientific success:** lower accepted-set risk at matched coverage than frozen strong baselines on the untouched endpoint.
